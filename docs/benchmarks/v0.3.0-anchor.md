# The v0.3.0 ARA anchor (the 2003 version)

**Status: complete (2026-11-25). The anchor MISSES.**
- **Routing load:** ARA 2003's routing load is far below AODV's at every
  pause time, in packets, in bytes and as NRL, so the gated NRL check (b)
  passes at pause 0 and 30 s.
- **Delivery:** ARA 2003's delivery is 13 to 16 pp below AODV's at pause 0
  and 30 s, well outside the pre-registered non-inferiority margin of 5 pp,
  so check (a) fails at both.

The rule needs (a) and (b), so the 2003 version's trend ([GS03] §5.3:
delivery alike, overhead lowest) is not reproduced in this harness. Under
v0.3.0's exit criterion (5) this page is the written infeasibility verdict.
Whether v0.3.0 ships on it is the maintainer's decision
([#125](https://github.com/swarm-routing/ARA/issues/125)).

## What was fixed before dispatch

The reading was accepted by the maintainer on 2026-11-25 on #125 (items
1-7), before the plan existed.
- **The plan:** `tools/bench/campaigns/anchor-2003.yaml` (#298), committed
  before its first dispatch.
- **Dependency checks:** `check_plan_deps.py` ended `plan deps: ok` at
  registration and again right before dispatch.
- **The expected miss** was written into the plan before dispatch (below).

- **Source.** Günes & Spaniol, *Ant-Routing-Algorithm for Mobile Multi-Hop
  Ad-Hoc Networks*, IFIP Net-Con 2003 ([GS03],
  <https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-35703-4_9>), §5.3. The trend: at high
  mobility ARA 2003 delivers about as well as AODV (both above DSR, which
  the org does not run), with the lowest routing overhead.
- **Arm "ARA 2003".** Every 2003 feature `core/` has, on:
  - `StatisticalPathSelection` (§2.2, §5.2; #135);
  - `FloodBackwardAnts` (§4.5; #136);
  - `PrioritizeAnts` (§4.5; #137);
  - `OneRetryOnFailure` (§4.3; #138);
  - `BufferDuringDiscovery`, the send buffer with the minimal FANT (§4.5; #5).

  Continual decrease is the default. Kept off: `DataAsFant` (#132), the
  2002 behaviour, and `LazyDecay` (#50), not from the paper. The MAC-Tap is
  absent (#139): a deviation, since [GS03] §5.2 credits it with "slightly
  better" delivery.
- **Cell.** anchor-3's field with Broch's traffic ([BMJ98], which [GS03]
  §5.3 cites):
  - `routing-compare --scenario=paper`: 50 nodes, 1500 m x 300 m, 300 m disk
    range, 900 s;
  - random waypoint with speeds U(0, 10) m/s (`--speedMin=0`);
  - 10 CBR flows of 4 packets/s of 64 B (`--cbrBps=2048`; [GS03] Fig. 6's
    "CBR 4x512Byte" read as 4 x 512 bit);
  - q = 0.1 (#140);
  - pauses 0, 30, 120 and 600 s;
  - ARA 2003 and stock ns-3 AODV in one invocation per seed, RngRun 1-20 per
    cell in two blocks of 10;
  - `ns3-dev:3.48-optimized` by digest (`sha256:5585a93...`; ns-3.49's AODV
    livelocks, swarm-routing/sim-containers#35, NS3-001).
- **Rule.** ARA 2003 minus AODV, paired by seed, 95 % CI:
  - (a) PDR: the CI's lower bound above -5 pp at pause 0 and at pause 30.
    This is a non-inferiority margin; delta = 5 pp is a repo choice for
    "alike", since [GS03] gives no number.
  - (b) NRL: the CI wholly below 0 at pause 0 and at pause 30.
  - Both must hold. 120 and 600 s, routing packets and `nrl_bytes` are
    reported, not gated.
- **Expected, stated before dispatch.** ab-2003-2's `all` arm (stat + flood
  + buf, at 1 packet/s) was -20.9 pp PDR below AODV at pause 0. If that
  carried over, the anchor would miss (a), with FloodBackwardAnts as the
  main cost (#136).

## Reading

- **Overhead: [GS03]'s claim holds.** ARA 2003 sends a small fraction of
  AODV's routing packets at every pause time (e.g. 15294.6 against 403157.0
  at pause 0), and its NRL and `nrl_bytes` CIs lie far below zero.
- **Delivery: [GS03]'s claim does not hold here.** ARA 2003 delivers 13 to
  16 pp less than AODV at pause 0, 30 and 120 s. The gap closes only near
  static (-2.82 pp at 600 s). That is the same direction as the 2002
  anchor's miss (`v0.2.0-anchor.md`), in a different traffic setting.
- **The switch A/Bs say where it comes from.**
  - FloodBackwardAnts lowered PDR by about 8 pp in every mobile cell of
    ab-2003-2 (#136), and undid the send buffer's gain when both were on.
  - StatisticalPathSelection was noise (#135).
  - BufferDuringDiscovery raised PDR by 5 to 11 pp.
  - ab-2003b (#137, #138, #132) is still being read; its verdicts land on
    those issues.
  - An arm without the flood is not this anchor's arm. The reading fixed the
    arm to the paper's feature set before dispatch, and changing it after
    the fact would be tuning to the result.
- **Deviations that could matter.** No MAC-Tap (#139); the 300 m disk radio
  instead of Broch's two-ray model; 64-byte packets; ns-3's 802.11 MAC and
  AODV, not the authors' simulator. None of them is shown to account for a
  13-16 pp gap.

## What it means for v0.3.0

Exit criterion (5) asks for this page: PASS, or a written infeasibility
verdict. This is the latter.
- The org rule 6 gates for ARA 2003 stay open: its delivery numbers are not
  presented as a reproduction of [GS03].
- No default moves on this anchor (criterion 7).
- Shipping v0.3.0 on it, as v0.2.0 shipped on its own miss, is the
  maintainer's decision (#125).
